WeWED Planner Assistant — Privacy policy

Prototype built by Team Khao Khao at the Rewired Hackathon, 19 Nov 2026.

What we collect

Your name, WhatsApp number, the workspace you join, and the messages, voice notes and photos you send to the WeWED bot, so we can turn them into tasks, reminders and proof for your wedding planner.

How we use it

Only to run task coordination inside your planner's workspace. Voice notes are transcribed by Sarvam AI and understood by Google Gemini. We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Who can see it

The planner of the workspace you joined. Other members only see their own tasks.

Delete your data

Send DELETE MY DATA to the bot on WhatsApp, or ask your planner to remove you. Your messages and tasks are removed from our database.

This prototype uses synthetic demo data.
